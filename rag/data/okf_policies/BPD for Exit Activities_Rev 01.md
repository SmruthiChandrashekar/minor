Purpose

The document details the process and procedures that an employee is required to follow in event of the employee’s voluntary separation from the organization.

Scope

The process and procedures apply to an employee voluntarily resigning from the organization.

Departmental Objectives

The exit process enables the employer to gather inputs on the employee’s experience in the organization. It also ensures the transfer of knowledge/ Knowledge Transition (KT) from the exiting employee to the assigned individual.

Responsibility

The primary responsibility of the process lies with the process owner (HC-Comp & Ben) and verified by the functional head. The process owner shall ensure that the exit process workflow is executed and adhered to as per the documented procedure.

Guidelines for Exit procedure

The Exit process detailed above shall be followed by any employee voluntarily leaving the organization i.e. being relieved from the Puravankara group in accordance with the terms and conditions of the employment agreement. 

Notice period & relieving 

When an employee resigns, the Reporting Manager (RM)/HoD/Business Head (Biz. Head) shall attempt to understand the reasons behind the resignation, identify and address corrective measures to retain the employee (if possible). 

In case it is not possible to retain the employee, the RM in concurrence with the HC Team finalizes the Last Working Day (LWD) or the relieving date for the resignee. The notice period to be served by the resignee will be as per the terms of appointment*:

During the notice period, the resignee as well as the Reporting Manager must ensure that the necessary/essential information, related to all the projects or tasks the employee has been working upon is handed over to the designated team member/ employee. 

Guidelines for Exit Interview

The Exit interview provides the exiting employee an opportunity to give feedback on the employee’s work experience in the organization. These inputs would be helpful in designing and implementing corrective measures to make the Puravankara group a great workplace. 

The HC team conducts the exit interview with the resignee 7 days prior to the LWD. 

The exit interview discussion with the resignee is documented and if required shall be shared with the Functional Heads and Executive Board.

Guidelines for Full & final settlement

The employee shall complete the clearance formalities and get the necessary sign-off on the clearance form from the specific departments. 
The clearance form with the necessary documents must be handed over to the HC on the last working day to enable computation of the full & final settlement. 
The employee’s Full & Final settlement will be processed upon receiving the approved resignation letter and signed clearance form from the respective departments. The Finance/HC team to ensure that the employee’s Full & Final settlements are carried out within 20 days from the Last Working Day, subject to receiving the duly completed clearance form.
