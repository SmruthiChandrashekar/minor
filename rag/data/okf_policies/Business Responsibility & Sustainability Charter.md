Business Responsibility and Sustainability Charter

Stakeholder Management 

Introduction 
Puravankara Limited (the “Company”) recognizes that effective stakeholder management is an integral our business operation. In order to understand the needs of stakeholders and to involve them in managing risks and resolving conflicts and to ensure that the long term ‘social license to operate’ for a business, a systematic structure to identify them and to establish sufficient modes of engagement with them is required. 

Scope 
Each business unit in every operating location is required to carry out stakeholder engagement in accordance with the principles of this and to ensure that they maintain and commit to play a role in nurturing the Company’s stakeholder relationships.
 
Objective 
Ascertaining the nature of impacts on stakeholders, their legitimate concerns, interests and expectations as well as their potential influence on the company.
Embedding the principles of inclusiveness, transparency, materiality, completeness and cultural appropriateness in all our engagement activities.
To ensure the disclosure of information about the company’s activities in a timely and culturally appropriate manner to allow for informed and meaningful engagement.
To ensure the stakeholder grievances are resolved in a timely and culturally appropriate manner.
Designing appropriate engagement methods and plans that meet international standards, are tailored to the stakeholders’ needs and have well-defined objectives and outcomes that can be documented and monitored.
Promoting awareness of the principles and procedures for stakeholder engagement with employees at various levels of our operations through training and communication.
Undertaking engagement in a non-discriminatory and interactive manner that encourages stakeholders to provide feedback and engage positively with the business operations.
To identify potential engagement risks. 

Stakeholder Identification and Prioritization 
Relevant stakeholders are those individuals, groups of individuals or organisations that affect and/or could be affected by an organisation’s activities, products or services and by the associated performance about the issues addressed by the engagement. An organisation may have many stakeholders, each with distinct attributes and often with diverse and conflicting interests and concerns. 
Establishing a methodology for systematically identifying stakeholder groups that can contribute to achieving the purpose of the engagement and/or could be affected by its outcome is fundamental to the engagement process.

A method for systematically identifying stakeholder groups should consider the scope of the engagement and may be guided by attributes of stakeholders such as the following: 
Dependency – groups or individuals who are directly or indirectly dependent on the organisation’s activities, products or services and associated performance, or on whom the organisation is dependent in order to operate.
Responsibility – groups or individuals to whom the organisation has, or in the future may have, legal, commercial, operational or ethical/moral responsibilities.
Tension – groups or individuals who need immediate attention from the organisation about financial, wider economic, social or environmental issues. 
Influence – groups or individuals who can have an impact on the organisations or a stakeholder’s strategic or operational decision-making. 
Diverse perspectives – groups or individuals whose different views can lead to a new understanding of the situation and the identification of opportunities for action that may not otherwise occur.

Stakeholder Engagement Process 
At Puravankara Ltd, we promote a culture of consistent engagement with every stakeholder group to seek their feedback in order to improve our business processes.
The stakeholder engagement process shall be used which further shall be subject to change as per the relevant circumstances. The basic overview of the engagement process shall take into consideration the profiling and mapping of stakeholders. 
The engagement process shall further develop a clear understanding of who the relevant stakeholders are and how and why they may want to engage with the organisation.
We strive to abide by the following principles when engaging with any stakeholder:
Inclusivity - Inclusivity is the participation of stakeholders in developing and achieving an accountable and strategic response to sustainability. An inclusive organisation accepts its accountability to those on whom it has an impact and who have an impact on it.
Materiality - Materiality is determining the relevance and significance of an issue to an organisation and its stakeholders. A material issue is an issue that will influence the decisions, actions and performance of an organisation or its stakeholders.
Responsiveness - Responsiveness is an organisation’s response to stakeholder issues that affect its sustainability performance, and is realised through decisions, actions and performance, as well as communication with stakeholders.
Common modes of engagement with Stakeholders:
The common modes of engagement with stakeholders shall, amongst other, be email, meetings, reports, website.

Responsible Advocacy 
1. Introduction
Puravankara Limited (“the Company”) recognizes that responsible advocacy and constructive engagement with government bodies, industry associations, regulatory authorities, and other relevant stakeholders are important for contributing to sustainable sectoral development and informed policy dialogue.
The Company seeks to undertake all advocacy-related activities in a manner that is aligned with its values of integrity, transparency, accountability, and ethical business conduct, while balancing the interests of diverse stakeholders.

2. Purpose
This Statement aims to:
Promote responsible and ethical advocacy practices aligned with the Company’s values, mission, and business objectives.
Provide guiding principles for employee engagement and representation before external stakeholders and authorities.
Encourage compliance with applicable legal, regulatory, and governance requirements in advocacy-related activities.
Support mechanisms for monitoring advocacy practices and addressing concerns relating to non-compliance.
Promote awareness of responsible advocacy practices among employees and relevant personnel.

3. Guiding Principles
The Company endeavours to follow the below principles in relation to advocacy and external engagement activities:
Advocacy activities shall be conducted in a lawful, transparent, ethical, and responsible manner.
The Company seeks to engage constructively with government agencies, industry bodies, non-governmental organisations, and other relevant stakeholders on matters relating to the sectors in which it operates.
Employees and authorised representatives are expected to avoid conflicts of interest and uphold the Company’s values while representing the organisation.

Appropriate systems and processes may be maintained to support compliance with this Statement and facilitate reporting or redressal of concerns relating to potential non-compliance.
Suitable corrective measures may be undertaken in cases of confirmed violations, wherever necessary.
Divisional Chiefs, operational heads, and relevant functional teams may support implementation of this Statement within their respective areas of responsibility.
The legal and compliance functions may monitor relevant advocacy practices and support compliance oversight, wherever applicable.

4. Communication and Awareness
The Company endeavours to communicate this Statement to employees and relevant stakeholders through appropriate internal communication mechanisms and awareness initiatives.

5. Review and Governance
This Responsible Advocacy Statement serves as a guiding framework for the Company’s advocacy-related practices and engagements. The Statement may be reviewed and updated by the management from time to time to ensure its continued relevance, effectiveness, and alignment with applicable business and regulatory requirements.

Environmental Committments
1. Introduction
Puravankara Limited (“the Company”) is committed to conducting its business in an environmentally responsible and sustainable manner. The Company recognizes the importance of integrating environmental stewardship into its business strategy, project lifecycle, and operational practices.
The Company endeavours to minimise environmental impacts arising from its developments and operations through efficient resource utilisation, adoption of sustainable technologies, climate-responsive practices, and continuous improvement in environmental performance. Through these efforts, the Company seeks to contribute towards a low-carbon and resource-efficient built environment while creating long-term value for stakeholders.
2. Purpose
This Statement seeks to provide a guiding framework for managing environmental impacts associated with the Company’s operations, projects, and value chain activities, while promoting sustainable development and responsible environmental practices.
3. Scope
This Statement is intended to apply across:
Business operations, projects, offices, and facilities of Puravankara Limited and its subsidiaries;
Employees, contractors, consultants, suppliers, and business partners associated with the Company; and
Various stages of project development, including design, construction, operations, maintenance, and redevelopment activities.
4. Environmental Commitments
 The Company endeavours to:
Comply with applicable environmental laws, regulations, and statutory requirements.
Promote sustainable construction practices and pursue green building certifications, wherever feasible.
Enhance energy efficiency and encourage the adoption of renewable energy solutions to support climate resilience and reduction of greenhouse gas emissions.
Conserve water resources through efficient usage, rainwater harvesting, recycling, reuse, and wastewater management practices.
Minimise waste generation by promoting resource efficiency, waste segregation, recycling, reuse, and responsible disposal practices aligned with circular economy principles.
Prevent and mitigate air, water, soil, and noise pollution arising from construction and operational activities.
Promote biodiversity conservation, ecological considerations, green landscaping, and native plantations across developments, wherever feasible.
Encourage sustainable procurement practices and engage with suppliers, contractors, and business partners that align with the Company’s environmental expectations.
Promote environmental awareness, accountability, and capacity building among employees, contractors, and stakeholders.
Continuously improve environmental performance through monitoring, innovation, adoption of sustainable technologies, and transparent sustainability-related disclosures.

5. Review and Governance
This Environmental Statement serves as a guiding framework for environmental management and sustainability-related practices across the Company’s operations. The Statement may be reviewed and updated by the management from time to time to ensure its continued relevance, effectiveness, and alignment with applicable business, operational, and regulatory requirements.

Supplier Code of Conduct
1. Purpose
Puravankara Limited (“the Company”) recognises its suppliers, vendors, contractors, consultants, service providers, and business partners as important stakeholders contributing to the Company’s growth and success.
This Supplier Code of Conduct Statement outlines the principles and minimum standards of ethical, social, environmental, and governance practices that the Company expects its suppliers, including their employees, affiliates, subcontractors, and representatives, to follow while conducting business with the Company.
Suppliers are expected to comply with applicable laws, regulations, and standards and conduct business in a responsible, transparent, and ethical manner.
2. Business Integrity and Ethics
Puravankara Limited expects suppliers to conduct business operations with integrity, fairness, and transparency. Suppliers are encouraged to:
Comply with applicable laws, rules, and regulations in the jurisdictions in which they operate.
Maintain high standards of ethical conduct in all business dealings.
Prohibit all forms of bribery, corruption, extortion, fraud, and money laundering.
Avoid actual or potential conflicts of interest in dealings with the Company and disclose such conflicts, wherever applicable.
Promote fair competition and refrain from anti-competitive practices.
Refrain from offering or accepting gifts, hospitality, payments, or benefits intended to improperly influence business decisions.
Maintain accurate books, records, and financial statements in accordance with applicable accounting standards.
3. Labour and Human Rights
The Company encourages fair labour practices and respect for human rights across its value chain. Suppliers are expected to:
Provide a workplace free from discrimination, harassment, abuse, or intimidation.
Prohibit child labour, forced labour, bonded labour, and all forms of modern slavery.
Comply with applicable laws relating to wages, working hours, benefits, and employment conditions.
Respect employees’ rights to freedom of association and collective bargaining in accordance with applicable laws.
Promote equal opportunity and fair treatment irrespective of gender, caste, religion, ethnicity, disability, age, or other protected characteristics.
Establish appropriate grievance redressal mechanisms for employees and workers.
4. Health, Safety, and Environment
Suppliers are encouraged to provide safe and healthy working environments and operate in an environmentally responsible manner. Suppliers are expected to:
Comply with applicable occupational health, safety, and environmental laws and regulations.
Identify, assess, and manage workplace health and safety risks.
Provide safe working conditions, relevant safety training, and necessary protective equipment to workers.
Minimise environmental impacts arising from operations through responsible management of energy, water, waste, emissions, and natural resources.
Obtain and maintain necessary environmental permits, approvals, and registrations, wherever applicable.
Promote sustainable business practices and strive for continual improvement in environmental performance.
5. Confidentiality and Data Protection
Suppliers are expected to safeguard confidential and proprietary information shared by the Company and ensure appropriate protection of data. Suppliers are encouraged to:
Maintain confidentiality of business information obtained during the course of engagement with the Company.
Comply with applicable data privacy and data protection laws.
Respect and protect the intellectual property rights of the Company and third parties.
6. Compliance and Monitoring
The Company may assess and monitor supplier adherence to this Statement through declarations, reviews, audits, or other appropriate mechanisms, wherever considered necessary.
Suppliers may be requested to provide relevant information and cooperate in connection with compliance assessments. In cases of identified gaps or non-compliance, suppliers may be encouraged to implement appropriate corrective actions within reasonable timelines.
7. Reporting of Concerns
Suppliers are encouraged to promptly report any actual or suspected violations of this Statement, unethical conduct, fraud, corruption, harassment, or non-compliance observed in connection with the Company’s operations or business relationships.
The Company endeavours to ensure that concerns raised in good faith are addressed appropriately and without retaliation.
8. Review and Governance
This Supplier Code of Conduct Statement serves as a guiding framework for responsible business conduct across the Company’s supply chain and business relationships. The Statement may be reviewed and updated by the management from time to time in line with applicable laws, evolving industry practices, and business requirements.
This Statement is intended to apply to suppliers and business partners engaged with Puravankara Limited.
