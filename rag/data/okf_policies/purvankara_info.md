Puravankara Limited is a leading Indian real estate company established in 1975.

The company develops residential and commercial properties across major cities like Bangalore, Chennai, and Kochi.

It operates under brands such as Purva and Provident Housing.

Puravankara is known for quality construction, innovation, and customer-centric development.
