Sub: The Inter-State Migrant Workmen (RE&CS) Act 1979 - Certificate of Registration
Regarding
With reference to your application received on 11.04.2025, Certificate of Registration is hereby issued subject to the
following statutory conditions:
1. Any change in particulars requires submission of application for Amendment.
2. Contractor must obtain Inter-State Migrant Licence and subsequent renewals.
3. Minimum wages under Minimum Wages Act 1948 must be disbursed in presence of principal employer's
representative.
4. Health and safety education must be imparted to migrant workers in their native language.
5. Accommodation provided must be well-ventilated, hygienic, and adhere to health protocols.
6. Mandatory Web Portal Upload: Employer/contractor must upload details of all Inter-State Migrant Workers to
https://labour.tn.gov.in/ism.
