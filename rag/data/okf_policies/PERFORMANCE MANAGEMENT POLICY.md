PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT 
POLICY 
 
1.OBJECTIVE 
 
• To evaluate the Performance of employees and align individuals’ goals towards achieving 
business objectives. 
• Identifying Training and Development needs in order to build competencies critical to job and 
to the position. 
• Reward performance and to build a platform for career development and promotions within the 
organization. 
• Helps a feedback system in the organization. 
• To provide every employee with the conditions an d opportunities to assume greater 
responsibilities in the organization 
 
 2.SCOPE 
 
 Applicable to employees at all grades who have joined on or before 30th September. 
 3. POLICY 
 
• Every year, the access to online Performance Appraisal will be given to respective reporting 
managers by the HR Department for a limited period . The online forms are d uly filed-in in all 
aspects and completed before the stipulated time frame. 
 Performance Rating Scores:- 
 Significantly Exceeded Expectations (SEE) = 5 
 Exceeded Expectations (EE) = 4 
 Met Expectations (ME) = 3 
 Met Some Expectations (MSE) = 2 
 Not Met Expectations (NE) = 1 
 
 The Head HR shall evaluate employees eligible for promotion vis-a-vis the rating given 
 by the reviewer keeping in mind the following aspects: 
• Performance shall be given more emphasis than seniority. 
• Apart from performance, appropriate weightage may be given to seniority, ability to work in 
other departments and academic qualifications acquired in the present level etc. 
• Once analysis on ratings is completed a list of eligible employees will be prepared in the order 
of their ranking. The Head HR will include his recommendations and forward the same to the 
Management for approval. 
• Promotions of employees will be based solely on the performance of an employee.

• Promotion at all levels in the organization s hall be done through Performance Management 
System – Performance Grid. 
• Final decision on appraisals / promotions will be at the discretion of the Management. 
 
4. PROCEDURE 
 
• Performance Grid - PMS access is given by HR Department to all eligible employees. 
• Every employee completes his Self-Appraisal in stipulated time. 
• HOD will enter his views on the employee’s performance. A joint meeting is scheduled between 
the Appraiser and the Appraisee, by the appraiser for this purpose. 
• Training Evaluation would be a part of the Performance Appraisal process. 
• Final review of Appraisal takes place between HOD and HR. 
• After review process gets completed, Appraisals feedback are analyzed by HR Department. 
• Analysis of Performanc e Ratings is sent to the HOD for normalization wherever required. 
Based on the analysis final increments and promotions are decided.

PROCESS FLOW FOR PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT 
Distribution of Forms/system access to Employees online 
 
Appraisal by the Reporting Manager 
 
Appraiser / Appraisee Discussions and Appraisal by 
HOD 
Review by HOD / CEO / Head HR 
Final Review and Normalization 
Increments / Promotions
