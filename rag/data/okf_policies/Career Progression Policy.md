PURAVANKARA | PROVIDENT | STARWORTH
 CAREER PROGRESSION POLICY
1. PURPOSE
The policy is for the Career Progression of staff working in the organization.
2. SCOPE AND ELIGIBILITY
The policy is applicable to all employees working across all locations.
3. POLICY STATEMENT
 Career Progression is defined as sequence of jobs and working experiences that an individual has over his or her
working life. These may be focused on developing depth or breadth of expertise, just as much as on upward
progression.
 The organization aims to provide opportunities to help individuals with their longer-term Career Progression.
 This policy is based on three principles:
 a) Responsibility for Career Progression is shared between individuals, managers, respective departments, and
Human Resources.
 b) Formal Career Progression opportunities will be open to those performing well in their current job.
4. CAREER PROGRESSION RESPONSIBILITIES
Responsibilities for career progression are shared between:
- Individuals
- Line managers
- Human Resources
- Executive Team
- Management
5. TALENT DEVELOPMENT FRAMEWORK
Talent Development aims to equip the organization to:
a) Develop an effective leadership culture.
b) Drive change, modernization, and transformation.
c) Respond to the needs of service users.
d) Identify staff potential at all levels of the organization.
e) Provide greater Career Progression opportunities.
f) Protect against succession risks.
g) Enhance the organization reputation as a great place to work.

CAREER PROGRESSION PROCESS & GUIDELINES

Talent Development relies on four levels of data for its success:
h) Individual performance level in the current role.
i)
Individual Career Progression aims.
j)
Organization needs in terms of future skills and succession.
k) Individual potential against these organizational needs.
a) EQUAL OPPORTUNITIES MONITORING
The organization is committed to equality and will undertake to monitor Career Progression activities
on the grounds of gender, ethnic origin, disability, and age. This is to ensure there is equality of
opportunity for all employees to the access and take-up of Career Progression activities to support the
implementation of our Equality and Diversity Policy. This information will be kept confidential by the
Human Resources team and will be used for no other purpose than to enable the organization to fulfil
its obligations for monitoring equal opportunities.
b)
RESPONSIBILITY

All members of staff referred to within the scope of this policy are required to adhere to its terms and
conditions.

Human Resources has the responsibility for ensuring the maintenance, regular review and updating of
this policy
