LOCAL CONVEYANCE POLICY 
 
Objective: 
 
The Conveyance Policy has been formulated to provide guidelines to employees while traveling for 
business purposes and to communicate process for approval, reimbursement, and management of 
travel expenses. 
 
Scope: 
 
All employees of Puravankara Group who need to travel to perform their official duties. 
 
Guidelines: 
 
1. Local Travel arrangements must be taken care of by the traveler (him/her). 
 
2. Wherever possible, employees are encouraged to use their personal vehicles for official duty, 
within the city limits. 
 
3. In the case of private transport, only OLA / Uber / Call Taxi / Pre-paid Taxis / Auto /Bus should 
be used as applicable. 
 
4. The Administration department will provide private vehicles on a need basis for official work 
outside of city limits, in which case Vehicle request Slip / Email, duly approved by the Reporting 
Manager, a day prior to travel date. 
 
5. Employees at Asst. Manager & junior levels to use two-wheeler or public transport. However, on a 
need basis, department heads can permit an employee to use Auto/Ola/Uber, as applicable. 
 
6. Women employees who work beyond 8.30 p.m. for any emergency work at the office shall be 
provided with transport and their stay must be informed through the respective departmental 
head before 4.30 p.m. to the Administration department. H owever, this facility will not be 
available to those who are on shifts. 
 
7. Employees at Dy. Manager and above levels would be eligible to use personal cars for official 
purposes. 
 
8. Duly approved Local Conveyance Reimbursement Form (LCRF), by the Reporting manager / 
HOD shall be submitted to the Finance department. The Finance department will release the 
reimbursement at the stipulated time. 
 
The revised rates of re-reimbursement effective from 1st November 2023 towards using Auto / Taxi / 
own vehicle for official work are as follows: 
 
Two-Wheeler : INR 5.50 / km. 
Four-Wheeler : INR 13.00 / km. 
Auto / Taxi : At actual 
 
Claim Reimbursement Process: 
 
Local Conveyance Reimbursements should be claimed on a monthly basis and the claims should be 
submitted to the Accounts by the 10th of the following month after obtaining due approvals.
