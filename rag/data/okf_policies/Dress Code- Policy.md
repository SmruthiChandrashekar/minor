POLICY GUIDELINES ON PROFESSIONAL WORK ATTIRE 
At Puravankara, we take pride in presenting a thoroughly corporate and professional 
demeanour at work. “Elegant and Smart Home Spaces” is what we build and sell, and that 
should be highly reflective in our professional attire. To articulate this in a very simple form, 
it means, adopting to appropriate and commonly accepted standards of dressing and 
grooming that reflects a positive image of the company, for our customers, business 
partners/associates and colleagues. 
Remember, every Puravankara employee is “A Brand Ambassador” of the company! 
In essence, we are redefining the general guidelines for professional work attire for both Men 
and Women. 
• Monday – Friday : Business Formal 
• Saturday : Smart/ Business Casual 
 
BASIC GUIDELINE TO PROFESSIONAL WORK ATTIRE 
MEN WOMEN 
MONDAY- FRIDAY(& during client meeting 
on weekends/holidays) 
• Business formals, muted and sober 
colours (Neatly ironed Trousers/ Full 
Sleeved Shirts preferably with Tie or 
Blazers), complete w ith belt, neatly 
maintained formal leather/Suede 
Shoes (Black/Brown) and Socks 
• Impeccably Groomed, with neat 
haircut, clean shaven (no -no to 
overnight stubble) or beards that 
are neatly trimmed 
 
• Not Appropriate work attire (and 
not limited to) low rise jeans/pants, 
T-Shirts (any type), Cargo Pants, 
rugby shir ts, floaters, sneakers, 
sandals or clothes that you would 
wear to a pub/night club 
MONDAY- FRIDAY 
• Business/Western Formals - Neatly 
ironed and well fitted trousers/ 
Classy blouses/ Shirts/ formal skir ts 
(of conservative length) 
• Indian Wear- Elegant Salwar Kameez/ 
Sarees (conservative prints and cuts) 
• Footwear- Comfortable stilettoes, 
closed shoes, peep toes, sandals 
• Well-groomed and professional 
 
 
Not Appropriate work attire( and not 
limited to) low rise Jeans/ Jeggings, Capris/ 
Cargos/ Tights, bling, 
miniskirts/Spaghettis/and clothes that make 
great beach or clothes that you would wear 
to a pub/night club 
 
SATURDAY (Men and Women) 
• Business casuals or smart casuals that includes jeans/formal capris (if there are no 
client/customer meetings 
• Employees in customer facing positions, are expected to exercise professional 
judgement while dressing casually on Saturdays especially when you have arranged 
for meetings with clients/ customers/associate
