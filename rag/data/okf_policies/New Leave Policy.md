LEAVE POLICY

importance of providing certain amount of flexibility to the employees to take care of 
personal domestic and social necessities. This leave policy is applied on calendar year 
basis (i.e period from 1st January to 31st December of a year), effective from 1st January 
2013.

EARNED LEAVE (E.L) 
1. All employees will earn 1 day for EL for every 20 days of physical presence/ 
attendance and credited monthly. 
 
2. Earned leave may be accumulated upto a maximum of 30days as of 1 st 
January. 
 
3. Unavailed earned leave beyond 30 days at the end of the calendar year , will 
automatically get lapsed (No encashment is allowed ) 
 
4. Encashment of earned leave is allowed onl y on resignation/ termination of 
service and only basic salary will be considered for earned leave encashment. 
 
5. Employees on probation will be entitled for earned leave on pro -rata basis 
computed from the date of joining and credited after completion of prob ation 
period or on confirmation of service and thereafter on 1st of every month. 
 
6. An employee may at any time, apply in writing to his/her departmental head / 
superior, at least 7(Seven days) in advance of the date from which he/she 
wishes to proceed on leave. The leave notice period can be reduced at the 
discretion of the leave sanctioning authority.

7. As far as possible, leave will be granted at the time applied for, subject to the 
exigencies of company`s business. Normally, it is expected that leave be 
planned in consultation with the departmental head/ superior to whom the 
employees is reporting. 
 
8. An employee has to take minimum of 3 days leave to avail E.L. However he/she 
may take EL in installments of lesser duration, purely at the discretion of t he 
departmental head/ superior, if he /she has no other leave at one`s credit, for 
a maximum of 3 such occasions each year. 
 
9. No fraction or less than 1-day leave shall not be allowed as E.L. 
 
10. If the leave requested by an employee is refused or postponed, the reasons for 
refusal shall be recorded in writing and communicated to the employees. 
 
11. If an employee, after proceeding on leave, desires an extension thereof, he/she 
shall apply in writing to his/her departmental head/ superior, who shall reply 
either granting or refusing extension of leave. 
 
12. Employees does not earn E.L., during leave without pay or maternity leave 
period. 
 
CASUAL LEAVE / SICK LEAVE (C.L / S.L.) 
 
1. Casual leave is intended for special circumstances that cannot be foreseen. 
2. Casual leave shall not exceed more than 2days. 
3. In general, prior permission of the departmental head / superior shall be 
obtained before such leave is availed. But, in case of an emergency, the 
departmental head / superior must be informed through an SMS / Email /phone 
call. 
4. No leave of any type may be combined with casual leave / sick leave. 
5. Casual leave / sick leave will be calculated on pro -rata basis and credited on 
1st January or on confirmation. 
6. Casual / sick leave cannot be carried forward and no encashment is allowed. 
 
LOSS OF PAY (LOP) 
1. LOP is not encouraged. 
2. LOP may be granted upto a maximum of 15days by the departmental head / 
superior. 
3. Provision for LOP is not a right of the employee. Prior approval is a must. 
4. LOP will be considered if the reason for availing is genuine, on a case to case 
basis. 
5. During LOP staff members are not entitled for any benefits.

MATERNITY LEAVE (ML) 
 
1. Female employees who have completed Eighty days in the twelve months will be 
entitled for this leave as per maternity benefits act. 
2. Female employees are eligible for ML up to a maximum period of 26 weeks / 182 
days on the basis of a medical certificate issued by a registered doctor. 
3. ML shall be applied 40 days in advance of the expected date of delivery. 
4. Those who are co vered under the employee’s state insurance act (ESI ACT) will 
be eligible for maternity benefits as per the ESI Act. 
5. In case of miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy, an employee shall, on 
production of the prescribed proof, be entitled to leave w ith wages at the rate of 
maternity benefits, for a period of 6 (six) weeks immediately following the day of 
her miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy. 
6. The application for the miscarriage leave should be supported with a copy of 
medical certificate from registered medical practitioner. 
7. Female employees will be paid Rs.3,500.00 towards medical bonus. 
 
 
COMPENSATORY LEAVE- WORKING ON SUNDAY / HOLIDAYS 
 
1. Only employees up to Sr. Site engineer / Sr. Executive are covered under this 
compensatory off policy. 
2. Those who are working on holidays i.e. Sunday / 2 nd Saturday of the month / 4 th 
Saturday of the month / public holidays to record their timings through time and 
attendance system. Working on these days to be approved by the departm ental 
heads / superiors. 
3. Employees who work for a full day , i.e., 8 hours Can avail full day leave and less 
than 8 hours or more than 4 hours can avail 1.5 day leave 
4. Attending office on Sundays and holidays to clear pending work / backlog shall not 
be considered for compensatory off. 
5. Compensatory off must be availed within 60days from the date of its accrual / 
eligibility. 
6. Compensatory off must not be availed for more than 2days continuously. 
7. Compensatory off must be availed with due approval from the superior / 
departmental head in advance.

UNAUTHORISED LEAVE 
 
1. Unauthorized leave (other than in emergencies) even for a day is subject to 
disciplinary action. 
2. If an employee remains absent unauthorized or overstays authorized or overstays 
the periods of leave originally granted or subsequently extended, for a period of 7 
consecutive days or more he/ she shall lose his / her appointment and would be 
deemed to have voluntarily abandoned his / her services and his/ her name will be 
struck off from the muster rolls. 
3. An employee who proceeds on leave and does not resume duties thereafter or 
submits resignation is treated as having voluntarily abandoned the service. The 
employee will lose right of leave and will lose the job. The resignation, at the 
company`s sole discretion, may be accepted from last working day in the company 
notwithstanding the leave granted earlier.
