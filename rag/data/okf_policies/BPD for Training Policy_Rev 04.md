Purpose

To develop a documented procedure for the activities of the Training Department & standardized process for imparting training in the organization
 
Scope

Applicable to all the employees across the Puravankara group and its subsidiaries

Departmental Objectives

Analyze, Design, Develop, Implement & Evaluate professional training programs & workshops that impact business results across the group

Responsibility

The overall responsibility for establishing, documenting, implementing and updating this procedure lies with the Learning& Development team. This procedure has been approved by Head-HC

6.1
Training Efficiency:
Employees identified/nominated for trainings/workshop/seminar based on the business requirement and approvals from Business Heads
Training Efficiency = Total requests executed/ Total number of requests received
(As per SLA, Training efficiency needs to be>= 80%)
Training Man-hours = No. of participants*No. of training hours
