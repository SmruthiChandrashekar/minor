Purpose 
 
 Create a culture of appreciation and recognition of performance across the Organization / Group. 
 
 
 Recognize Top Talent who has made a major impact through their performance/deliverables 
 
 
 Reward high performers who have contributed significantly in line with the annual strategy theme 
 
 
 Motivate people to aspire to contribute above and beyond their core KRAs

Impact Areas 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Employees making an impact towards the RISE 10K goal in any of the following areas would be eligible for nomination by their Dept Heads. 
Speed, agility and out of box thinking in driving “exceptional” performance, execution and delivery in a time bound manner is expected.

Process of Selection 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Recommend 
 End of every quarter and annual at end of FY.
 Dept Heads to share nomination with HR team and 
respective CEOs citing
- What exceptional/significant contribution 
the employee has done during the quarter 
- how the employee has made an impact 
towards the RISE 10K goal 
 Refer the detailed note on the guiding principles. 
Recommend using template with supporting 
docs/data in support of the nomination
Review 
 4 selects from the larger entities and couple from 
corporate functions and smaller entities
 Since the award recognizes outstanding 
contribution towards making an impact, the 
number of awardees to be diligently looked at.
 CEO and HR to evaluate each case on merit and 
vis-à-vis other nominations.
Recognize 
 
 HR will conduct a Recognition and Rewards 
session to facilitate the awardees
 CEO and Dept Heads to recognize the 
awardees
 Communication announcing the awardees

• Recognizing those who have made an impact

• Awarded to exceptional few, not to be a mass give-away

Nomination guidelines

•

Refer the Impact areas; identify the most applicable one

03 What it is not

Recognition is NOT for doing their core/expected work

• All Depts. Not expected to have recommendation 
based on above criterias 
06 Sanity of the process: 
•

Kindly maintain confidentiality till further communication 
Kindly do not inform the nominee or others till the 
nomination is approved for recognition
