Sub: Memorandum of Understanding (Medical Service Tie-up)
Dear Sir,
We are in progress for construction of building project at Provident Ecopolitan Aerospace Bangalore,
Huvinayakanahalli Village since January 24. We are expecting around 800 to 1000 work force at the peak level of the
project. For this reason, we need medical service tie up with your hospital as per the terms & conditions given below:
01. Admission & Referral: The employee / dependent should produce referral letter from the authorized signatories
at the time of admission. In case of emergency, temporary credit facility will be provided on production of identity card
issued by Kalpataru Projects International Ltd. In case of failure to produce the referral letter within 24 hours, the
employee shall be responsible to settle the bills at the time of discharge.
02. Medical Bills & Payment: The hospital shall raise medical bills along with the photocopy of the referral letter and
discharge summary on the respective units of Kalpataru Projects International Ltd. On receipt of the bill from the
hospital, the company shall settle the bill within the credit period of 30 days after the deduction of applicable TDS.
Delay of payment beyond the period of credit will attract interest of 9% P.A. and will lead to stoppage of credit facility.
03. Rate Revision: If any rates are revised, the hospital will intimate the same in writing to Kalpataru Projects
International Ltd.
04. Termination: This agreement can be terminated by either party by giving one month of prior notice.
05. Biomedical Waste: Site Biomedical Wastage will be collected and disposed to Hospital who have tie up with
Medicare Bio Medical Management.
06. Term / Validity: This MOU will come into force from 10.02.2025 and will be in force till 09.02.2026. This MOU
shall be void unless renewed at the end of the above period.
