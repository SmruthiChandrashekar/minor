Employee Relocation Policy
There may be business demands that might require employees to be relocated from one location to another from time
to time. While such movements are part of any growing organization, as an employee-friendly company, the company
will provide support and assistance to the extent possible towards making such relocations hassle-free.
Objective:
This policy is designed to assist employees and / or new hires who are being transferred or requested to be relocated
by the company to support business needs. Assistance provided includes shifting of household goods, temporary
accommodation, and travel arrangements. All assistance provided is at the discretion of the Company and facilitated
by Administration.
Scope: This policy is applicable across the entire group.

b. Travel:
✓ All travel arrangements will be in line with the Domestic Travel Policy. Family members (spouse and children) are
eligible for the same mode of travel.
✓ Family details must be provided along with the Relocation Request Form (RRF).
c. Accommodation:
✓ Temporary accommodation arranged in line with the travel policy up to a maximum of 15 days from the date of
relocation.
✓ Daily Allowance is not applicable in relocation cases.
d. Movement of Household Articles:
✓ Shifting of household goods including motor vehicle (if any) arranged by Admin Department within permissible
limits.
✓ Employee submits list of items with three quotations from packers and movers.
✓ Admin department negotiates and finalizes vendor.
✓ Amounts are exclusive of taxes, levies, and insurance, but inclusive of service charges.
e. Expense Reimbursement (RRCF):
✓ Approved Relocation Reimbursement Claim Form (RRCF) with invoices/tickets submitted within 10 days.
✓ Final claim form must be submitted within 15 days of relocation.
Approvals & Eligibility:
1. Relocation allowance applies only when transfer is company-initiated to meet business demands.
2. Not applicable when transfer is initiated on employee's personal request.
3. Management reserves the right to modify or withdraw terms.
Recovery Clause:
If an employee voluntarily resigns within 12 months from the relocation date, the employee must reimburse the entire
relocation cost paid by the company, recoverable from Full & Final Settlement.

Annexure-I: Relocation Request Form (RRF)
Form Fields: Name, Emp. Code, Designation, Mobile, Email, Company, Department, Project, Location, Moving From,
Moving To, Travel Date, Mode of Travel, Family Details (Name, Relationship, Age/DOB), Accommodation Required
(Yes/No, Dates), Goods Shifting (Yes/No), Admin Vendor Approvals.
Annexure-II: Relocation Reimbursement Claim Form (RRCF)
Form Fields: Name, Emp. Code, Designation, Mobile, Company, Project, Department, Relocation From/To, Expense
Details Table (Sl.No, Bill No, Date, Narration, Amount), Debit Split (% and Amount per Company), Signatures of
Employee, Reporting Manager, Business Head, Admin, Finance.
