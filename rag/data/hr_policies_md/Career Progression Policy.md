# Puravankara Group - Career Progression Policy

**Brands**: Puravankara | Provident | Starworth  
**Scope & Eligibility**: Applicable to all employees across all company locations.

### 1. Purpose
The policy provides structured guidance for the career progression of staff working across the organization.

### 2. Policy Statement & Principles
- Career progression is a sequence of jobs and professional experiences aimed at deepening expertise or moving upward.
- Core principles:
  1. Shared responsibility across individuals, line managers, departments, and Human Capital.
  2. Formal progression opportunities are tied to proven performance.

### 3. Key Responsibilities
- **Individuals**: Drive self-learning, identify career aspirations, maintain performance standards.
- **Line Managers**: Conduct regular reviews, offer constructive developmental feedback, mentor team members.
- **Human Resources**: Provide career frameworks, training infrastructure, and objective appraisal systems.
- **Executive & Management**: Align business growth opportunities with internal talent mobility.

### 4. Talent Development Framework
- Cultivating strong leadership culture and succession readiness.
- Driving transformation and identifying potential across junior and senior staff.

a) EQUAL OPPORTUNITIES MONITORING 
 
The organization is committed to equality and will undertake to monitor Career Progression activities 
on the grounds of gender, ethnic origin, disability, and age. This is to ensure there is equality of 
opportunity for all employees to the access and take-up of Career Progression activities to support the 
implementation of our Equality and Diversity Policy. This information will be kept confidential by the 
Human Resources team and will be used for no other purpose than to enable the organization to fulfil 
its obligations for monitoring equal opportunities.
