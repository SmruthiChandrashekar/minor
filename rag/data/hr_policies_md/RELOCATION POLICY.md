# Puravankara Group - Employee Relocation Policy

### Objective & Scope
Designed to assist employees and new hires relocated to support business needs. Covers shifting of household goods, travel, and temporary stay across the Puravankara Group.

---

| Band | Allowance Amount |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Violet** | **Actuals** |
| **Indigo** | **Rs. 60,000/-** |

*Note: Slabs are exclusive of taxes/levies/insurance.*

---

### Key Policy Clauses
1. **Travel**: In accordance with Domestic Travel Policy. Spouse and children eligible for the same mode of travel.
2. **Temporary Accommodation**: Provided for up to a **maximum of 15 days**. Daily allowance is *not* applicable.
3. **Household Goods & Vehicle Shifting**: Coordinated via Admin Department. Employee must submit itemized list with 3 packers & movers quotes.
4. **Reimbursement Deadlines**:
   - Vouchers and bills submitted within **10 days**.
   - Final Relocation Reimbursement Claim Form (RRCF) submitted within **15 days**.
5. **Eligibility**: Applies strictly to company-initiated transfers. Employee-requested transfers for personal reasons do not qualify.
6. **12-Month Recovery Bond**: If an employee resigns within **12 months** of relocation, 100% of the relocation expenses paid by the company are recovered from their Full & Final settlement.
