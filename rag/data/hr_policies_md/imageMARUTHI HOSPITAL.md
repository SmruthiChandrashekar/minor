# Kalpataru & Sri Maruthi Hospital - Memorandum of Understanding (Medical Service Tie-up)

**Reference**: COR/KPIL/PEAB/K/03/2024-25  
**Date**: 10th February 2025  
**Project**: Provident Ecopolitan Aerospace Bangalore (Huvinayakanahalli Village)  
**Workforce Covered**: 800 to 1,000 workers  
**Validity Period**: 10.02.2025 to 09.02.2026  

### Hospital Partner
- **Hospital**: Sri Maruthi Hospital ("Sri Guru Krupa Nilaya")
- **Address**: Bagalur, Bangalore North - 562149

### Key Terms & Conditions
1. **Admission & Emergency Credit**: Employees/dependents must produce an authorized referral letter at admission. In emergencies, temporary credit is granted on showing the KPIL Identity Card (referral letter required within 24 hours).
2. **Billing & Credit Terms**: Bills submitted with referral letters and discharge summaries. KPIL settles within 30 days after TDS. Delayed payments past 30 days attract 9% p.a. interest.
3. **Rate Revision**: Hospital must give written notice of any revised medical rates.
4. **Notice Period**: 1-month prior written notice required for termination by either party.
5. **Biomedical Waste**: Site biomedical waste collected and disposed via Medicare Bio Medical Management tie-up.
6. **MOU Period**: Valid from 10.02.2025 until 09.02.2026 unless renewed.
