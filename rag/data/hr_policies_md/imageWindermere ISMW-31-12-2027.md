### Statutory Conditions
1. **Mandatory Portal Upload**: All Inter-State Migrant Workers must be registered on the Tamil Nadu Labour portal (`https://labour.tn.gov.in/ism`).
2. **Minimum Wages**: Wages must be disbursed in the presence of the Principal Employer's designated representative in compliance with the Minimum Wages Act, 1948.
3. **Safety & Health in Native Language**: Safety and health training must be delivered in the migrant workers' native language.
4. **Housing Standards**: Hygienic, well-ventilated accommodation must be provided with health protocols.
